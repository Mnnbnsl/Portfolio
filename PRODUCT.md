# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, engineering managers, open-source collaborators, and potential clients who visit the portfolio to evaluate Manan Bansal's developer experience, technical skillset, and code quality.

## Product Purpose
A premium, highly polished personal portfolio representing Manan's identity as an AI & Full-Stack Developer. It showcases real-world projects and skills to build credibility and open professional channels.

## Brand Personality
- Technical & Minimalist
- Precise & Raw
- Focused & Premium

## Anti-references
- Saturated AI-generated templates (card grids with generic icons repeated endlessly).
- Decorative gradient text (`background-clip: text` gradients) and glassmorphism.
- Neon blue-purple SaaS templates.
- Repetitive uppercase tracked eyebrows on every single section.
- Section numbers (01 / 02 / 03) used as default decoration rather than sequential flow.

## Design Principles
- **Functional Contrast**: Use strict visual and color contrast (e.g., `#050605` canvas and `#edf0e6` ink text) to maximize readability over raw decoration.
- **Rhythmic Space & Typography**: Rely on solid font pairings (Inter and JetBrains Mono) with carefully scaled typography and line lengths (65–75ch) for high readability.
- **Meaningful Motion**: Motion is treated as a component feature, utilizing smooth transitions and marquee effects with complete reduced-motion support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Maintain strict contrast ratios (>= 4.5:1) for all body, muted, and placeholder texts.
- Full support for `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` disabling non-essential transitions.
- Clear native/custom focus outlines for all interactive navigational elements.
